## About Plavex

Plavex is built for courier operations that need a single dashboard for day-to-day delivery management. It supports dispatch workflows, shipment visibility, payment handling, and customer communication so teams can coordinate service without juggling separate tools.

It is especially useful for last-mile delivery setups that need to keep drivers, shipments, and finance records aligned. The platform also includes a branded customer tracking page that does not require login, making it easier for recipients to check shipment status directly.

---

## Features

- Dispatch drivers from a central operations view
- Track shipments live on a map
- Manage COD and payment records
- Reconcile driver payouts against delivery activity
- Send messages and updates to customers
- Monitor on-time performance across deliveries
- Provide a branded customer tracking page with no login required
- Support courier and last-mile delivery workflows in one platform

## Usage

1. Open the dashboard and review current shipments and driver status.
2. Assign drivers to pending deliveries from the dispatch area.
3. Track active shipments on the map as they move through the route.
4. Record COD or payment details as deliveries are completed.
5. Reconcile payout totals against completed jobs and driver activity.
6. Send shipment updates to customers when status changes.
7. Share the branded tracking page so recipients can check delivery progress without signing in.
